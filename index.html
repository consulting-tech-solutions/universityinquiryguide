Popular Study Destinations Overview

Popular

  • • Post-Study Work: 2 Years Graduate Route
  • • Top Focus: Business, Tech, Law, Healthcare

United States

STEM Focus

Intakes: Fall (Aug/Sep) & Spring (Jan)

  • • Degree Duration: 2 Yr Master's / 4 Yr Bachelor's
  • • Post-Study Work: Up to 3 Years (OPT for STEM)

Australia

High PR Path

Intakes: Feb / March & July / Aug

  • • Degree Duration: 1.5-2 Yr Master's / 3 Yr UG
  • • Post-Study Work: 2 to 4 Years Subclass 485
  • • Top Focus: Nursing, IT, Accounting, Construction

Europe & UAE

Affordable

Intakes: Sep/Oct (Europe) & Jan/Sep (Dubai)

  • • Degree Duration: 1-2 Yr Master's

  • • Top Focus: Int'l Business, AI, Management

Application & Admission Timeline

Phase 1

Initial Profile Assessment & Test Prep (Months 1-2)

Finalize course choices, appear for IELTS/PTE exams, and gather academic transcripts & recommendation letters.

Phase 2

University Application & SOP Drafting (Months 3-4)

Draft Statement of Purpose (SOP), submit applications to 3-5 target universities, and apply for early scholarships.

Phase 3

Offer Acceptance & Financial Documentation (Months 5-6)

Receive conditional/unconditional offer letters, deposit tuition fees, and prepare bank statements for visa filing.

Phase 4

Visa Processing & Pre-Departure (Months 7-8)

File student visa application, complete medical checkups, book accommodation, and attend pre-departure briefing.
